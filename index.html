Is this legal advice?

No. The IEP Ready Kit is an educational resource only. It does not give legal advice and does not replace advice from a licensed attorney, an advocate or your school's special education team.

Is it for every state?

Yes. The kit is general guidance that applies to IEP meetings across the United States. Your state may have its own rules, so check your state's education department website for details specific to you.

What is an IEP?

An Individualized Education Program is a written plan that a US public school creates for students who qualify for special education services under IDEA. The kit explains the basic terms.
