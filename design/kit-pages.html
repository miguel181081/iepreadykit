IEP Meeting Checklist

Work through this list in the weeks before the meeting, on the day, and right after. Tick each box as you go.

Two weeks before
Confirm the date, time and who will attend
Ask for copies of any new reports or evaluations
Re-read the current IEP and last progress report
Fill in the Parent Concerns Worksheet
Decide if someone will come with you
The day before
Pick your top 3 priorities
Mark the questions you most want answered
Put everything in one folder or binder
During the meeting
Write down each person's name and role
Share your child's strengths first
Bring up your top 3 concerns
Ask how each goal will be measured
Ask for time to review before you sign
Right after
Write a short summary while it's fresh
Send the follow-up letter within a few days
Add goals to your tracking sheet

Questions to Ask

Circle the questions that matter most to you. Leave space to write the answer.

Who will provide each service?
How many minutes a week, and where?
